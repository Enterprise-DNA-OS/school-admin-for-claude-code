---
description: The Monday review for the principal and business manager, written from compliance, debtors, absences and enrolments.
---

```bash
npm run school -- weekly-review --json
```

Write it in four short parts: 1. Child safety and care (compliance breaches, by name). 2. Attendance (unexplained days past the window). 3. Fees (total overdue, the oldest accounts, who is on a plan). 4. Enrolments (places left by year group, lapsed offers, quiet enquiries). Finish with who should act on each, and nothing else. Save it to drafts/ as weekly-review-YYYY-MM-DD.md if the operator wants to share it.

Every answer starts from the CLI output. Use the record codes it prints. Never answer about a family, student or staff member from memory, and never invent a record. If a name matches more than one record, show the candidates and ask.
