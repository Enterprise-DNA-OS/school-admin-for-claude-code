# Why this target: research and the six scores

Checked 8 November 2026.

Synergetic, from Education Horizons, is a school management system used by independent schools in Australia and New Zealand. The vendor describes one system for finance, administration, student records, fundraising, community engagement and property, with over 40 modules to choose from ([product page](https://educationhorizons.com/solutions/synergetic)).

**Price.** Education Horizons does not publish prices. The only public figure is a review-site listing of "starting from US$5,000", priced per feature ([GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/education-childcare-software/a/synergetic)). That is not a vendor quote or a school's invoice, and it does not say what the figure covers. No savings claim is made here.

**Export.** The vendor documents extracting data with Microsoft Query, debtor reports, and CSV exports for government reporting ([Synergetic help](https://synergetic.help.tes.com/support/solutions/articles/75000140879-stats-export-explained), [debtors overview](https://synergetic.help.tes.com/support/solutions/articles/75000145081)).

| Score | 1 to 5 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Annual bill | 3 | Module-priced, quoted privately; schools license several modules. No verified invoice. |
| Data-shaped | 4 | Families, students, fees, attendance and checks are tables and weekly routines. Timetabling and teacher screens are not. |
